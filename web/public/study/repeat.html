When an ARP goes back to the same class

ARPs often observe the same class again a few months later, and often the same teacher. If the form records what goes on in a classroom, two forms for one class should agree more than two forms for different classes. This page checks that. A class here is one school, one grade and one subject (Hindi or Maths) on the Grade 1–3 FLN forms, and each later visit is paired with the visit before it, in an earlier month.

What this means for checking ARPs' forms

  • A form says more about the ARP who filled it in than about the class they watched. The same ARP's forms for two different schools match almost as well as two of their forms for one class.
  • A return visit by the same ARP is a weak check on an earlier form, since the ARP's habits repeat wherever they go.
  • Two ARPs who observe one class months apart agree no more than two ARPs who observe different classes in the same block. Either classrooms change a lot from month to month, or the form records little about the classroom. Two people observing the same lesson on the same day would tell these apart.
  • Nothing here shows classes improving between visits, or failing to. A form that tracks the classroom this loosely can't measure that either way.
