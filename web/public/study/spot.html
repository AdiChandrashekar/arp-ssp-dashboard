Spot assessments · Grades 1–5

What ARPs record in spot assessments

On a school visit, an ARP tests a few children and records each child's level: Saksham, Madhyam, Pragatisheel, or a zero score. This page looks at those results across months, districts, classes and ARPs. The data has no assessor column, so each school's results for a month are credited to the ARP who visited that school most often that month.
