Inside the FLN forms

The Grade 1–3 FLN forms are the core of supportive supervision: the Sandarshika, teaching material, the weekly tracker, the NIPUN Talika, workbooks and remedial work. This page looks at how ARPs answer them. Every comparison keeps grades and subjects apart, so a difference never comes from observing a different grade.

Grade barely changes the answers

Share of forms marking each practice as seen, by grade. You might expect Grade 3 classrooms to look different from Grade 1. On most questions the three grades sit within 1–3 points of each other.
